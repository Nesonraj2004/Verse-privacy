Delete Your Verse Account

Verse Team, operated by Neson Raj S

You can request deletion of your Verse account and the personal data associated with it. This page provides an external way to submit an account deletion request.

Request account deletion

Send an email from the email address associated with your Verse account to verse.helpdesk@gmail.com with the subject Verse Account Deletion Request.

In the email, include the email address used for your Verse account. We may ask for reasonable verification before processing the request in order to protect your account.

What will be deleted

After a valid deletion request, Verse will delete or anonymize data associated with your account, including where applicable:

  • Your Verse account and profile information
  • Campus/account-verification information linked to your account
  • Social connections and account-linked preferences
  • User-generated content and media that can be associated with your account, subject to applicable technical and legal limitations
  • Push-notification identifiers linked to your account

Information that may be retained

Limited information may be retained where reasonably necessary for security, fraud or abuse prevention, dispute resolution, enforcement of our rules, or legal and regulatory obligations. Where retained, the information will be kept only for as long as necessary for those purposes.

In-app deletion

If an account deletion option is available in your version of Verse, you may also start deletion from the account or settings area in the app. The external email method above remains available if you cannot access the app.

Questions

For questions about account deletion or privacy, contact verse.helpdesk@gmail.com.
