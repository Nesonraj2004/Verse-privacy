Verse Child Safety Standards

Verse Team, operated by Neson Raj S

Zero tolerance for child sexual abuse and exploitation

Verse strictly prohibits child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), child sexual abuse material (CSAM), grooming, sextortion, sexualisation of minors, trafficking of children, and any content or conduct that facilitates or promotes the abuse or exploitation of children.

1. Prohibited content and conduct

Users may not create, upload, share, request, distribute, promote, or facilitate any content or behaviour involving the sexual abuse or exploitation of a child. This includes CSAM, grooming, sexual solicitation of minors, sextortion, trafficking, or attempts to move abusive activity off-platform.

2. In-app reporting and safety tools

Verse provides in-app reporting and blocking tools so users can report accounts, messages, conversations, or user-generated content that may violate our safety standards. Reports are reviewed and appropriate action may be taken against violating content or accounts.

3. Handling CSAM and child-safety reports

When Verse obtains actual knowledge of suspected CSAM or other child sexual abuse or exploitation, we may remove or restrict the content, suspend or terminate relevant accounts, preserve information where legally required, and take other appropriate safety measures.

4. Reporting to authorities

Verse is committed to complying with applicable child-safety laws and regulations. Confirmed or legally reportable CSAM may be reported to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) or another relevant regional or law-enforcement authority, as required by applicable law.

5. Child safety point of contact

Google Play and other authorised parties may contact the Verse child-safety point of contact at verse.helpdesk@gmail.com. This contact is used for child-safety, CSAE and CSAM-related notices, enforcement questions, and urgent safety concerns.

6. Enforcement

Violations of these standards may result in content removal, feature restrictions, account suspension or termination, and reporting to relevant authorities where required.

7. Updates

These standards may be updated as Verse develops its safety systems or as legal and platform requirements change.
