Privacy Policy for Verse

Effective date: 8 October 2026
Last updated: 8 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Verse is operated by Neson Raj S. Verse collects, uses, stores, shares, and protects information when you use the Verse mobile application.

Verse is a campus-focused social application intended to help verified college students connect with their campus community through features such as profiles, Moments, chats, groups, campus content, events, Shuffle, Rush, reactions, reports, and notifications.

Privacy contact: verse.helpdesk@gmail.com

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Account and verification information

When you create or use a Verse account, we may process information such as:

  • Email address, including a college email address where required for verification
  • Authentication and account identifiers
  • College or campus affiliation
  • Verification status
  • Account creation and sign-in information

We use this information to create and secure your account, authenticate you, verify campus eligibility, and restrict campus-specific features to eligible users.

1.2 Profile information

You may provide profile information including:

  • Display name
  • Profile photo or avatar
  • Bio
  • Course, program, major, department, or joining year
  • Other profile information you choose to provide

Depending on the feature, profile information may be visible to other eligible Verse users.

1.3 User-generated content

Verse may process content that you choose to create, upload, send, or share, including:

  • Photos and videos
  • Moments or Stories
  • Campus posts and updates
  • Group posts
  • Captions, comments, reactions, and other text you submit
  • Event-related content
  • Files or documents where supported

Content visibility depends on the feature. Content may be shared with Mates, group members, eligible users from the same campus, or other users participating in the relevant feature.

1.4 Messages and chat information

Verse provides communication features including personal or Mutual Moment conversations, group chats, campus-related communication, and session-based features such as Shuffle. To operate these features, we may process:

  • Message text
  • Sender, recipient, group, and session identifiers
  • Message timestamps and delivery metadata
  • Images, videos, voice messages, or files sent in supported chat features
  • Group membership and participation metadata

Private message content is not intentionally used for product analytics. Unless Verse explicitly states otherwise for a specific feature, you should not assume that a conversation is end-to-end encrypted.

1.5 Mates, groups, events, and campus activity

We may process:

  • Mate or social-connection relationships
  • Group memberships and participation
  • Events viewed and RSVP status
  • Campus activity needed to provide campus-only features

1.6 Shuffle

When you use Shuffle, Verse may process:

  • Campus eligibility
  • Selected interests or preferences
  • Match and session identifiers
  • Session status and Mutual Reveal choices
  • Safety actions such as Skip, Stop, Block, or Report

Shuffle is designed to match eligible users within the applicable campus community. Shuffle message content is not intentionally used for product analytics.

1.7 Rush

For campus Rush, Verse may process:

  • Campus membership
  • Rush session participation and timing information
  • Push-notification eligibility and delivery information
  • Activity metadata required to operate a time-limited Rush session

1.8 Reports, blocking, and safety information

If you report a user or content, we may process:

  • The identifier of the reported account or content
  • The report category or reason
  • Details you choose to provide
  • Relevant safety, moderation, and abuse-prevention metadata

Block relationships may be stored to enforce restrictions between users.

1.9 Device permissions and selected media

Depending on the feature you use, Verse may request permission to access:

  • Camera — to capture photos or videos you choose to create
  • Microphone — to record video, audio, or voice messages where supported
  • Photos/media library — to select media you choose to upload
  • Files/documents — to select supported files you choose to attach
  • Notifications — to deliver messages, Moments, group activity, Rush, and other app notifications

Verse uses these permissions only when needed for the related feature and subject to the permissions you grant through your device.

1.10 Push notification information

To deliver notifications, Verse may process:

  • Expo push token or equivalent device push identifier
  • Notification preferences
  • Delivery status and error information
  • App/device metadata necessary for push delivery

If a push token is reported as invalid or no longer registered, it may be removed from our systems.

1.11 Product analytics

Verse uses PostHog for product analytics. We may collect limited information such as:

  • App opens and session activity
  • Canonical screen names
  • Feature usage and successful product actions
  • App version and build number
  • Platform and operating-system information
  • A pseudonymous internal user identifier, such as a user UUID

Our analytics configuration is designed not to intentionally send:

  • Private chat or Shuffle message text
  • Campus Update body text
  • Moment or Story captions
  • Report descriptions
  • Email addresses
  • Authentication tokens
  • Signed storage URLs
  • Uploaded file contents or storage object keys

1.12 Crash and diagnostic information

Verse uses Sentry for crash and error monitoring. Diagnostic reports may include:

  • Error type and stack trace
  • App version and build number
  • Device platform and operating-system version
  • Technical diagnostic context
  • A pseudonymous internal user identifier where applicable

Our crash-reporting configuration is designed to avoid intentionally transmitting private messages, authentication credentials, uploaded content, or other sensitive user-generated content.

2. How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • Create, authenticate, and maintain accounts
  • Verify campus eligibility
  • Provide profiles, Moments, chats, groups, campus features, events, Shuffle, and Rush
  • Store and display content you choose to share
  • Deliver push notifications
  • Operate safety, reporting, blocking, and moderation features
  • Prevent spam, fraud, abuse, and unauthorized access
  • Measure product usage and improve Verse
  • Diagnose bugs, crashes, and reliability problems
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations

3. How Information Is Shared

3.1 With other Verse users

Information and content that you choose to share may be visible to other Verse users according to the feature's audience and access rules, such as Mates, group members, campus users, or matched users.

3.2 Service providers

Verse currently relies on service providers including:

  • Supabase — authentication, database, realtime, and backend infrastructure
  • Cloudflare R2 — storage and delivery of uploaded media and files
  • Expo / EAS / Expo Push — mobile application infrastructure, builds, and push delivery
  • PostHog — product analytics
  • Sentry — crash and error monitoring

These providers may process information only as necessary to provide their respective services and according to their own contractual and privacy obligations.

3.3 Legal and safety reasons

We may disclose information when reasonably necessary to:

  • Comply with applicable law or valid legal process
  • Protect users from harm
  • Investigate fraud, abuse, or security incidents
  • Enforce our rules or protect the rights and safety of Verse, users, or others

3.4 Business transfers

If Verse is involved in a merger, acquisition, financing, restructuring, or sale of assets, information may be transferred as part of that transaction subject to applicable law.

Verse does not currently sell personal data.

4. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide Verse, maintain accounts, operate safety and moderation systems, resolve disputes, prevent abuse, and meet legal obligations.

Different categories of information may have different retention periods. Some user-generated content may remain until deleted by you, removed by Verse, expired by the applicable feature, or deleted as part of account deletion.

Limited records may be retained where reasonably necessary for security, fraud prevention, abuse investigations, legal claims, or compliance with law.

5. Account and Data Deletion

You may request deletion of your Verse account and associated personal data by contacting: verse.helpdesk@gmail.com.

Please send the request from, or clearly identify, the email address associated with your Verse account so that we can verify the request. We may request reasonable verification before deleting an account.

After a valid deletion request, we will delete or anonymize personal information associated with the account, except for limited information that we may need to retain for security, fraud prevention, legal obligations, dispute resolution, or enforcement of our policies.

If Verse provides an in-app account-deletion option, you may also use that option directly.

6. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards designed to protect information from unauthorized access, misuse, alteration, loss, or disclosure. These safeguards may include authentication, access controls, database security policies, encrypted network transport, restricted backend permissions, and limited administrative access.

No method of internet transmission or electronic storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Your Choices and Controls

You may have controls including:

  • Device permission settings
  • Notification settings
  • Profile settings
  • Blocking and reporting tools
  • Leaving supported groups
  • Deleting supported content
  • Requesting account deletion

You may revoke camera, microphone, media, file, or notification permissions through your device settings, but some Verse features may not function without the permissions they require.

8. Children and Age Eligibility

Verse is designed primarily for college and higher-education communities and is not intentionally designed for young children. Users must meet the minimum age and eligibility requirements applicable to Verse and their jurisdiction.

If we learn that personal information has been collected from a child in violation of applicable law, we will take appropriate steps to delete it.

9. International Data Processing

Verse and its service providers may process or store information in countries other than the country where you live, including locations where our infrastructure, analytics, crash-reporting, or storage providers operate. Where required, appropriate safeguards are used for international processing.

10. User-Generated Content and Safety

Verse contains user-generated content and social communication features. Users are responsible for content they choose to share. We may remove content, restrict accounts, or take other safety actions when content violates applicable law, safety requirements, or Verse rules. Reporting and blocking tools are provided where supported.

11. Advertising and Future Paid Features

Verse does not currently use third-party advertising SDKs to display banner, interstitial, or native advertising. If our advertising practices change, we will update this Privacy Policy and relevant store declarations.

Verse may introduce optional subscriptions or in-app purchases in the future. Paid features do not necessarily mean that Verse contains advertising.

12. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as Verse evolves. When material changes are made, we may update the “Last updated” date and provide additional notice where appropriate.
